🔑 Access Control & Department Scoping

★ Full Access Users

Super Admin, Finance, & HR view all employee records across all departments without restriction.

🏢 Department Scoped Users

Department Managers (e.g. BRAC IED) view and export data scoped strictly to their assigned unit.

📥 Data Upload Specifications

Upload CSV or Excel files (.xlsx/.xls) in the left sidebar. Files auto-parse PIN, Gender, TIN, Department, Monthly Basic, Allowances, Arrears, and Tax Challans.

💡 Need Template Files? Click 📥 Sample Files in the top header bar to download pre-formatted CSV templates.

📄 Live PDF Preview & On-the-Fly Editing

Click PDF Statement on any employee row to open the live preview window. Type in custom Employee Name or Designation fields in the modal header—the PDF updates live in real time!

📊 Exporting Excel Summaries

Click 📊 Export Excel Summary to generate a formatted multi-sheet Excel workbook containing Employee Salary Summaries and Tax Challan Logs.

👑 Admin User & Scope Management

System Admins can click 👑 Manage Users & Scopes in the header to create new user accounts, assign roles, and grant department access scopes with persistent JSON storage.
